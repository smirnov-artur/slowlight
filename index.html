01 / THE CORE

EIGHT MINUTES OLD.
A HUNDRED THOUSAND
YEARS IN THE MAKING.

γ · 26.7 MEV

This is the story of the light that is hitting you right now.

It begins as violence. In the core, two hundred and fifty billion atmospheres of pressure hold hydrogen close enough to fuse. Four nuclei become one helium, and the missing mass leaves as light: a gamma photon, furious and newborn, in the one place the sun keeps darkest. It knows nothing. It will spend the next hundred thousand years learning the way out.

05 / THE SKY

WHY THE SKY
IS BLUE

Eight minutes of nothing, then everything at once: the atmosphere. Air is nearly transparent — nearly. Blue light, at four hundred and fifty nanometers, scatters about six times more strongly than red at seven hundred. The sky is blue because it is full of sunlight that didn’t make it straight through. Every clear day is a story of ten thousand small collisions, retold above your head.
